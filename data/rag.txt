Retrieval-Augmented Generation, commonly called RAG,
combines information retrieval with text generation.

A RAG system first retrieves relevant information from
an external knowledge source. The retrieved information
is then provided to a language model as context.

RAG can reduce hallucinations because the model can
ground its response in retrieved information.

A typical RAG pipeline contains document ingestion,
text chunking, embedding generation, vector storage,
retrieval, and answer generation.
